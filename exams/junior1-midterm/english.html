七年级英语上学期期中模拟卷

广州越秀口径 · 新课标 · 满分 100 分 · 建议用时 90 分钟
范围：上学期前半段（be 动词、代词、一般现在时、现在进行时、there be、校园与日常话题）· 自编模拟，非学校原题

注意事项：1. 先通读全卷再作答。2. 书写工整。3. 第Ⅰ大题可由家长按文末材料朗读；不便朗读时按中文提示直接填空。

Ⅳ. 阅读理解（30 分）

A

Jack is a Grade 7 student. He usually gets up at 6:40. He has breakfast at home and then walks to school. Classes begin at 8:00. Jack likes science best because it is useful. After school, he often plays basketball with his friends. On Sundays, he visits his grandparents. Right now, Jack is sitting at his desk. He is writing an email to his pen friend.

  1. What time does Jack usually get up?（2 分）
    A. At 6:00.　B. At 6:40.　C. At 7:00.　D. At 8:00.
  2. How does Jack usually go to school?（2 分）
    A. By bus.　B. By bike.　C. On foot.　D. By car.
  3. Why does Jack like science?（2 分）
    A. Because it is easy.　B. Because it is useful.　C. Because his teacher is funny.　D. Because he has no homework.
  4. What is Jack doing right now?（2 分）
    A. Playing basketball.　B. Visiting his grandparents.　C. Having breakfast.　D. Writing an email.
  5. Which is TRUE?（2 分）
    A. Jack is in Grade 8.　B. Jack never plays basketball.　C. Jack visits his grandparents on Sundays.　D. Jack is talking with his pen friend now.

B

Dear Mary,

How are you? I am in a new school this term. There are many subjects. I like English and P.E. best. My English teacher is kind. We have six classes on weekdays. After class, I often read in the library. Sometimes I play games with my classmates on the playground.

My new friend is Lily. She is from Beijing. She usually helps me with my Chinese. Now we are doing our homework together in the classroom.

Please write to me soon!

Yours,
Tom

  1. Tom likes ________ best.（2 分）
    A. Chinese and maths　B. English and P.E.　C. science and art　D. history and music
  2. How many classes does Tom have on weekdays?（2 分）
    A. Four.　B. Five.　C. Six.　D. Seven.
  3. Where does Tom often read after class?（2 分）
    A. In the classroom.　B. In the library.　C. At home.　D. On the playground.
  4. Lily usually helps Tom with ________.（2 分）
    A. English　B. maths　C. Chinese　D. P.E.
  5. What are Tom and Lily doing now?（2 分）
    A. Playing games.　B. Reading in the library.　C. Having classes.　D. Doing homework.
