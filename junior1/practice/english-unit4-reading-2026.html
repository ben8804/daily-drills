Ⅰ. 阅读理解 A（10 分，每题 2 分）

My name is Wang Fang. I am in Grade 7. I have classes from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM. In the morning, I have Chinese, maths and English. In the afternoon, I have history, IT and PE.

My favourite subject is English. It's useful, and my English teacher is really nice. She often lets us listen to English songs. I want to be an English teacher in the future.

I like PE too, because it is exciting. But history is difficult for me. I can't always remember the things in the past. My classmate Tom is good at history, and he often helps me with this subject.

  1. Wang Fang has classes from ________.
    A. 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM　B. 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM　C. 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM　D. 8:00 PM to 4:30 AM
  2. What classes does Wang Fang have in the afternoon?
    A. Chinese, maths and English.　B. History, geography and PE.　C. History, IT and PE.　D. French, music and science.
  3. Why is English Wang Fang's favourite subject?
    A. Because it is exciting.　B. Because it is useful, and her English teacher is nice.　C. Because she is good with numbers.　D. Because it is boring.
  4. What does Wang Fang want to be in the future?
    A. A singer.　B. A scientist.　C. An English teacher.　D. A music teacher.
  5. Which is TRUE?
    A. Wang Fang thinks PE is boring.　B. Wang Fang is good at history.　C. Tom often helps Wang Fang with history.　D. Wang Fang doesn't like English.

Ⅱ. 阅读理解 B（10 分，每题 2 分）

Hello, my name is Lucy Davis. I am a student in Canada. This term, I have many subjects: maths, biology, geography, French, music and science.

I like all the subjects, but my favourite is biology. The reason is that I love plants and animals. The class is not boring at all. It's exciting to learn about life. I want to be a scientist in the future.

My friend Baker is good with numbers, so his favourite subject is maths. He can work out maths problems quickly. It looks like magic! He often helps me with maths after class.

I also play an instrument on Friday afternoon. Music makes me happy, but I don't want to be a singer. I just like it for fun.

  1. Lucy is a student in ________.
    A. China　B. Canada　C. France　D. England
  2. Lucy's favourite subject is ________.
    A. biology　B. maths　C. music　D. French
  3. Why does Lucy like biology?
    A. Because she is good with numbers.　B. Because she wants to be a singer.　C. Because she loves plants and animals.　D. Because the class is boring.
  4. Baker is good with ________.
    A. instruments　B. numbers　C. French　D. animals
  5. Which is TRUE?
    A. Lucy's favourite subject is maths.　B. Baker often needs Lucy's help with maths.　C. Lucy wants to be a singer in the future.　D. Lucy plays an instrument on Friday afternoon.

家长对答案（打印自动隐藏）

Ⅰ 1. A　2. C　3. B　4. C　5. C

Ⅱ 6. B　7. A　8. C　9. B　10. D
