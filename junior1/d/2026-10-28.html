三、英语（约 12 分钟）

薄弱强化：一般现在时 / 现在进行时 / 第三人称单数 + 阅读定位

一、语法选择（每题 1 分，共 6 分）

1.Look! The boy ______ football on the playground.
A. play　B. plays　C. is playing　D. played
2.My sister ______ to school by bike every day.
A. go　B. goes　C. is going　D. went
3.—Where is Tom?
—He ______ his homework in his room.
A. do　B. does　C. is doing　D. did
4.She usually ______ milk for breakfast, but today she ______ juice.
A. drink; drink　B. drinks; is drinking　C. is drinking; drinks　D. drinks; drinks
5.The baby ______ now. Please be quiet.
A. sleep　B. sleeps　C. is sleeping　D. sleeping

A. don't; eat　B. doesn't; eats　C. doesn't; eat　D. don't; eats
