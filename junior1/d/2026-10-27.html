A. 语法选择（6 题）

1.—______ she ______ breakfast every morning?
—Yes, she does.
A. Do; have
B. Does; have
C. Does; has
D. Is; having
2.Look! The children ______ football on the playground.
A. play　B. plays　C. are playing　D. is playing
3.He ______ TV at the moment. He ______ his homework.
A. isn't watching; is doing
B. doesn't watch; does
C. isn't watch; is do
D. don't watch; is doing
4.My sister usually ______ to school, but today she ______ a bus.
A. walk; takes　B. walks; is taking
C. walks; takes　D. is walking; take
5.They ______ English every weekday.
A. study　B. studies　C. are study　D. studying
6.Be quiet! Grandpa ______.
A. sleeps　B. sleep　C. is sleeping　D. sleeping
